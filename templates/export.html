Single PDF file only

One image per page, delivered as a .zip. Higher DPI means sharper but larger images (30–600).

Extracts the PDF's text layer to a .txt file, with a --- Page N --- separator between pages. Pages that carry a text layer are extracted directly; scanned or image-only pages have none and come out empty — unless you enable OCR below to read them as images.
