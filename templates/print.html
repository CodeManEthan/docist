🖨️ PDF Print Prep

Impose a PDF for printing: fit several pages on a sheet (N-up) or lay it out as a fold-and-staple booklet.

📄
Click to select a PDF or drag it here
Single PDF file only

Places 2 or 4 source pages, scaled to fit and centered, onto each output sheet in reading order (left→right, top→bottom). Great for handouts and saving paper.

Reorders and pairs pages (2-up landscape) as a saddle-stitch signature, padding to a multiple of 4 with blanks. Print duplex, flip on the short edge, and fold in half to get a correctly ordered booklet you can staple along the spine.
